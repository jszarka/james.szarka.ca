What changes when I show up

01

Someone is accountable for reliability

Service level objectives that match what the business actually feels, on-call people can sustain, and incident reviews that change the system rather than blame the operator.

02

Releases stop being events

Pipelines that build the same way every time, environments defined in code, containers and orchestration where they earn their keep, and a rollback path nobody has to think about at 2am.

03

The cloud bill has an owner and a story

Spend attributed to teams, the obvious waste right-sized first, and governance so it does not quietly climb back next quarter. At TransAlta that was about $180K USD a year.

04

The team gets better, not just busier

Engineers who can run what they build, follow-the-sun coverage instead of heroics, documentation people actually open, and enough senior judgement in the room to say no to the wrong project.
