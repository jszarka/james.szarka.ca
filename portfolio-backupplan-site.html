BackupPlan.ca Business Website

BackupPlan.ca is a business website concept focused on backup planning, disaster recovery, business continuity, and protecting customer data before an outage becomes a crisis.

Backup Planning Disaster Recovery Business Continuity Data Protection

Project Goal

The goal was to create a professional service page that explains backup and recovery value in business language. The content needed to connect technical depth with practical customer outcomes: less downtime, safer data, and clearer recovery expectations.

Work Completed

  • Defined messaging for backup planning, recovery readiness, and business continuity consulting.
  • Created a page structure that supports future service pages, audits, and contact form workflows.
  • Used lightweight static-site delivery to keep the website simple, fast, and easy to host.
  • Added the project to the main James Szarka portfolio to show entrepreneurial and technical delivery experience.

Professional Value

This project highlights long-term experience in backup, DR, cloud storage, and operational resilience. It also shows how infrastructure leadership can be translated into a focused business offer with practical customer value.
